03 / What we look for

Few companies.
A real role in each.

Polanco is not a fund and does not run a broad portfolio. We look for a small number of companies where capital and hands-on work both matter, and where we can take responsibility for part of the outcome.

Stage

Operating, and approaching a decision.

An operating business or a defined asset, approaching a financing, a listing, a strategic partnership or a step up in scale. We take meaningful minority positions.

Sectors

Asset-heavy, real-economy businesses.

Our work so far has been in energy, food systems and circular materials: businesses with physical assets, real operations and a clear path to commercial scale. We stay open to adjacent sectors where the same work applies.

Geography

Canada and the United States.

Companies in Canada and the United States, with capital and commercial relationships that reach the Gulf and Latin America.

Involvement

A position and a role.

A meaningful position and a defined role: corporate development, capital strategy or a direct senior seat. We do not take passive positions.

What Polanco is not: a fund, a broker-dealer or a placement agent. We invest our own capital and do not manage third-party capital or arrange securities offerings. Co-investors, where involved, invest directly and on their own account. Investors and specialist partners are welcome to connect; an introduction does not imply access to an investment offering.

Do you invest in every company you advise?

Investment and advisory involvement are considered case by case. Neither is a requirement or a promise of the other. The role follows the opportunity and the agreed mandate.

Can Polanco work directly inside our company?

Yes. For selected companies, Polanco takes a direct corporate development and strategy role. Responsibilities and the level of involvement are defined for the engagement.

How can investors and specialist partners connect?

Share your focus, relevant experience and the kind of relationship you want to explore. We can then assess where there may be a fit. An introduction does not imply access to an investment offering.

04 / Where we contribute

The decision.
The plan. The work.

A financing, a strategic partnership or a new phase of growth starts with the same question: what does the business need to be ready?

01

Corporate development & strategy

Define the next stage, evaluate strategic options and coordinate the work across leadership, shareholders and external partners.

  • Growth priorities and strategic planning
  • Partnership and transaction evaluation
  • Direct corporate development roles where agreed
02

Capital strategy & readiness

Connect capital needs to the company’s operating plan and prepare a clear, supportable investment narrative.

  • Capital requirements and financing readiness
  • Corporate structure and materials coordination
  • Coordination with qualified professional advisers
03

Capital markets positioning

Help leadership explain the business, its milestones and its next stage to capital markets audiences.

  • Public market and listing readiness strategy
  • Investor narrative and company positioning
  • Specialist adviser selection and coordination
04

Strategic relationships

Identify relevant counterparties and prepare the company for purposeful conversations, with context and clear objectives.

  • Strategic and commercial counterparties
  • Investor relationship development
  • Research and communications specialists

05 / Who we are

The people at
the table.

Polanco Ventures is led by its founder, supported by a small team and an advisory circle, with specialist advisers brought in for each mandate. When Polanco takes a role inside a company, this is who shows up.

Founder & Managing Director

Santiago Herrera

Santiago founded Polanco Ventures in 2025 to invest in a small number of companies and work inside them. Before that he spent four years at Fortuna Investments, a Vancouver venture capital firm investing its own capital, where he built relationships with family offices and high-net-worth investors across Canada and the United States and oversaw corporate projects including private aviation acquisitions and real estate transactions.

Through Polanco he holds a direct Strategy and Corporate Development role at Denovia, a senior corporate development role at Sea to Sky Farms, and works with Robinson Energy on its capital markets positioning. He is also co-founder and chief executive of Outer Insights, a software company that gives private and public companies investor data intelligence and the workflow to build relationships with financing partners.

Santiago is a Canadian and Mexican citizen. He lives in Dubai and spends regular time in Mexico City, and works in English and Spanish. He represented Canada on the national taekwondo team from 2016 to 2017.
