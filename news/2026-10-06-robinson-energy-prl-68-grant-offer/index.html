Robinson Energy receives
a Grant Offer for PRL 68.

6 October 2026 · Robinson Energy

Robinson Energy has received a Grant Offer from Papua New Guinea’s Minister for Petroleum for Petroleum Retention Licence 68, the Triceratops gas and condensate discovery.

Robinson Energy announced on 6 October 2026 that it has received a Grant Offer from Papua New Guinea’s Minister for Petroleum for Petroleum Retention Licence 68, covering the Triceratops gas and condensate discovery in the Gulf Province. The offer precedes the formal grant of the licence. Once granted, PRL 68 would give Robinson a 100% interest for an initial term of five years, with a US$52 million work program that includes at least one appraisal well.
