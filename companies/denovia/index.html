The company

Denovia Inc. is a United States company developing a depolymerisation process for polyester waste, including textiles and PET, with pilot operations in Vancouver, Canada.

Polanco’s role

Polanco is an investor in Denovia and holds a direct Strategy and Corporate Development role. Our involvement spans strategy and corporate development as the business advances toward commercialisation.
