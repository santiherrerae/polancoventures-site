Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts, images, scripts and video are served from this domain, and the page makes no requests to third-party services until you submit the contact form.

When you submit the contact form, your name, work email, company and message are sent to a small service that Polanco Ventures runs on Cloudflare. It relays them as one email to contact@polancoventures.com through Resend, an email delivery provider, and keeps no copy. Cloudflare and Resend process the data as our providers under their own terms. We use your details only to reply to you. We keep the email for up to twelve months after our last exchange, unless a working relationship follows, and we do not add you to any mailing list. To have it deleted sooner, write to contact@polancoventures.com.

If that service is unavailable, the form prepares the email in your own mail application instead, and nothing is sent until you send it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. Polanco Ventures does not receive or use those logs.

Links to the companies’ own sites and to LinkedIn lead to services with their own terms and privacy practices.

Questions about this page can be sent to contact@polancoventures.com.
